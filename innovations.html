Innovation in action

Innovation isn't just words at Codebridge — it is action. In partnership with Core Group Ltd, we bring hands-on electronics and technology sessions to schools and student clubs across Kigali, guiding learners through real circuit-building, coding and robotics — and celebrating what they build.

School programme

Ingobyi Program

Learners in the Ingobyi Program were introduced to hands-on electronics, guided through simple circuit-building activities. The session closed with student presentations and a certificate ceremony celebrating their achievement.

3rd place — Edify 2026

La Plaisir d'Enfant School

A technology club set up for the school's learners, introducing them to circuits, coding boards and teamwork. Their dedication paid off when the club placed 3rd at the Edify Competition 2026.

School partnership

St Emmanuel Complex School

Our team worked closely with staff and student representatives, sharing knowledge and strengthening our partnership in technology education for the students.

Hands-on electronics

World Mission High School

Students engaged directly with circuit boards, laptops and microcontrollers in the school library, applying what they learned through guided, hands-on electronics sessions.

Vacation camp

Light Center Kabuga

We joined students at Light Center Kabuga as they wrapped up their vacation camp, celebrating the season together with certificates and smiles.
